# Yapn approved redesign
User approved the "Bright and friendly" direction (2026-10-14), replacing the earlier near-black and cyan page because it looked generic, did not say clearly what Yapn does, did not feel credible and felt too cold for small business owners. Audience: any small business owner, industry-neutral but concrete. Warm off-white paper #FCFAF6, ink #1C1A40, logo indigo #3630A4 as the strong accent, cyan and violet from the logo used as soft tints. Bricolage Grotesque for display, Figtree for body. Rounded color blocks, pill buttons with a pressed shadow, and sticker chips naming what Yapn does on a call. No em dashes anywhere. No pricing, stats, performance numbers or guarantees. No testimonials, customer logos, ratings, dashboard screenshots or phone numbers. Example calls and campaigns must be labeled as examples. Only claim real capabilities: answering questions, phone orders, texting links, emailing leads, transfers to the team, outbound confirmation and reminder campaigns with a text follow-up, and call logs with summaries. Real logo only (dark-wordmark copy on light surfaces), login https://dashboard.yapn.ai. One real contact form: name, email, optional company, message, to team@yapn.ai. Site CSS in src/app/globals.css is the authoritative token and visual system.
